How to Submit Your 10 Evidence Photos

Follow these steps to submit your waste management participation portfolio to GVAHS SHS Research.

1

Collect 10 Photo Evidences

Take photos of your waste segregation, recycling, or cleaning activities around campus/home. You can submit up to 10 different photos.

2

Fill Details & Upload

Enter your Full Name and Grade & Section (e.g., 11 - Mt. Caraballo). Click 'Select Multiple Evidence Photos' or fill slots 1-10 individually.

3

Instant Cloud Sync

Even 50MB to 100MB camera images compress instantly in your browser before syncing directly to the Admin Dashboard for instant verification!

GVAHS Research Requirement: Ensure your face or student ID is visible in at least one of your evidence photos so the research admins can match points accurately.
